### Progressive hints

#### Product of Array Except Self

- Hint 1 - small directional nudge: For each index, separate "product before me" from "product after me".
- Hint 2 - relevant invariant or data-structure choice: One pass can store left-side products in the answer; a second pass can multiply in the right-side product.
- Hint 3 - high-level approach, still without code: Sweep left to right while carrying the product of prior values, then sweep right to left while carrying the product of later values.

#### Subarray Sum Equals K

- Hint 1 - small directional nudge: A subarray ending at the current index has sum k when an earlier prefix is current_prefix - k.
- Hint 2 - relevant invariant or data-structure choice: The map stores how many times each prefix sum has appeared before the current index.
- Hint 3 - high-level approach, still without code: Initialize the zero prefix count, scan once, add the number of matching prior prefixes to the answer, then record the current prefix.
