### Progressive hints

#### 3Sum

- Hint 1 - small directional nudge: After sorting, treat one value as fixed and ask which pair must be found to complete zero.
- Hint 2 - relevant invariant or data-structure choice: For each anchor, the left and right pointers scan only the suffix after the anchor, and duplicates must be skipped at both levels.
- Hint 3 - high-level approach, still without code: Sort, iterate anchors, use two pointers to find complementary pairs, record each unique triplet, and skip repeated anchor/left/right values after a match.

#### Container With Most Water

- Hint 1 - small directional nudge: The current area is limited by the shorter of the two chosen lines, not the taller one.
- Hint 2 - relevant invariant or data-structure choice: Since width only decreases, keeping the shorter line cannot produce a larger area with any inner partner.
- Hint 3 - high-level approach, still without code: Start at both ends, update best area, then move the pointer at the shorter line inward until the pointers meet.
